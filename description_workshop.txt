This is an illusion, a challenge forged from obsession...
As an add-on module for Fargo's Souls Mod, this mod attempts to blend the masochistic features of previous iterations with its whimsical ideas elements, aiming to deliver a "distinctive" experience.
Currently, the mod only modifies some bosses though additional content may be introduced in the future.

This is an illusion, a challenge forged from obsession...
As an add-on module for Fargo's Souls Mod, this mod attempts to blend the masochistic features of previous iterations with its whimsical ideas elements, aiming to deliver a "distinctive" experience.
Currently, the mod only modifies some bosses though additional content may be introduced in the future.


I apologize if this feels too challenging. However, this is precisely how the mod was designed. It reflects the creator's warped taste, so I kindly ask for your understanding and compromise. You are a brave champion, and your spirit is stronger than you realize. No matter what horrors or malice assail you, "those things" cannot kill you. Your conviction is undoubtedly true and unwavering. As long as you don’t give up, you possess the strength to keep moving forward, dispelling any nightmare in your path.
